## 1. Executive Summary & Core Mandate
Inflation Video V2 was rebuilt from the ground up under a strict creative and educational directive:
> **ONE SHOT. ONE IDEA. ONE CLEAR VISUAL FOCUS.**  
> *Explain it so a curious 10-year-old understands intuitively.*

The prior version (V1) suffered from excessive information density: competing subtitles, 3 separate category cards, multi-line footer paragraphs, micro percentage tags, and academic economics vocabulary (*"nominal value"*, *"purchasing power erosion"*, *"nominal stability masks real decay"*).

In V2, we preserved all high-value visual assets (the 3D banknote, the physical grocery basket, the balance scale, the warm ivory lighting, and the multi-track procedural soundtrack) while radically decluttering the composition and simplifying the narration.
