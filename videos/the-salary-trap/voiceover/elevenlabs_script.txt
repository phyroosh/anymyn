A person earning forty thousand rupees a month can be just as broke as someone earning thirty thousand.

Say you earn thirty thousand rupees, spend twenty-seven thousand on living expenses, and save three thousand. You have a small safety cushion.

Then, you get a raise. Your salary jumps to forty thousand rupees. You expect your savings to instantly shoot up.

Instead, lifestyle creep quietly takes over. A slightly nicer apartment costs four thousand more. Ordering food more often takes three thousand. A new phone adds another three thousand.

At the end of the month, your total spending is thirty-seven thousand rupees. And your savings? Still exactly three thousand.

You didn’t get richer. Your life just got more expensive. Whenever your income grows, save half the raise first. Upgrade your life with the rest.
