## 2. Key Terms & Phrasing

- **"lifestyle creep"** (`/ˈlaɪfstaɪl kriːp/`): Speak naturally as a single compound idea, without emphasizing "creep" in a spooky or bizarre manner. Treat it as a recognized psychological phenomenon.
- **"safety cushion"** (`/ˈseɪfti ˈkʊʃən/`): Soft, warm tone; conveys peace of mind.
- **"And your savings?"**: Rise slightly in pitch on "savings" to create an inquisitive, conversational question mark before the silence.
- **"save half the raise first"**: Firm, decisive, memorable cadence. Make this the definitive takeaway of the video.
