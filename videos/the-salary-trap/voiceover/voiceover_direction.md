# ANYMYN Voiceover Direction Guide
## Topic: Why a Higher Salary Doesn't Always Make You Richer
**Slug:** `the-salary-trap`  
**Word Count:** 124 words  
**Estimated Spoken Duration:** ~50 – 54 seconds  
**Target Delivery Rate:** 135 – 145 words per minute (deliberate, cinematic, conversational)

---

## 1. Voice Profile & Character

- **Archetype:** Intelligent, warm, conversational, authentic financial documentary narrator.
- **Tone:** Engaging, calm, confident, and deeply relatable. Think of a trusted mentor explaining an eye-opening life realization over coffee.
- **Strict Exclusions:**
  - ❌ Avoid hyped, breathless YouTube-influencer shouting.
  - ❌ Avoid robotic, monotonic corporate presentation delivery.
  - ❌ Avoid finger-wagging, preachy, or morally judgmental tones.

---

## 2. Emotional Arc & Beat-by-Beat Progression

1. **Beat 1 (0.0s – 5.5s) — The Paradox:**
   - *Emotion:* Intriguing, calm, matter-of-fact confidence.
   - *Delivery:* Deliver the opening statement clearly without hesitation. Emphasize **"forty thousand"** and **"just as broke"**.

2. **Beat 2 (5.5s – 14.0s) — The Grounded Baseline:**
   - *Emotion:* Clear, comfortable, educational.
   - *Delivery:* A relaxed, rhythmic cadence establishing the baseline budget. Let the numbers feel ordinary and familiar.

3. **Beat 3 (14.0s – 24.5s) — The Raise & Anticipation:**
   - *Emotion:* Optimistic, relatable excitement.
   - *Delivery:* Slight upward vocal inflection on **"raise"** and **"shoot up"**. Capture the natural human feeling of getting a promotion.

4. **Beat 4 (24.5s – 36.5s) — The Creep (Upgrades Arrive):**
   - *Emotion:* Eye-opening, insightful, non-judgmental revelation.
   - *Delivery:* A structured tripartite rhythm: item one (apartment), item two (food delivery), item three (phone). Insert a crisp 250–300ms pause between each item to let the visual props land on screen.

5. **Beat 5 (36.5s – 44.0s) — The Climax & Silent Realization:**
   - *Emotion:* Sobering, visceral realization.
   - *Delivery:* Pivot sharply on **"And your savings?"** with an inquisitive lilt. Hold a **deliberate 400ms pregnant silence** before delivering the punchline: **"Still exactly three thousand."** Let the silence do the heavy lifting.

6. **Beat 6 (44.0s – 53.0s) — The Verdict & Empowering Blueprint:**
   - *Emotion:* Masterful, inspiring, empowering clarity.
   - *Delivery:* Deliver the two-part punchline with conviction: **"You didn't get richer. Your life just got more expensive."** Then warm up into an encouraging, actionable finish: **"save half the raise first. Upgrade your life with the rest."**

## 4. Key Words for Emphasis

- *"A person earning **forty thousand rupees** a month can be **just as broke**..."*
- *"...spend **twenty-seven thousand**... and save **three thousand**."*
- *"...lifestyle creep **quietly takes over**."*
- *"...nicer apartment... ordering food... a new phone..."*
- *"...And your savings? **Still exactly three thousand**."*
- *"You didn’t get richer. **Your life just got more expensive**."*
- *"Whenever your income grows, **save half the raise first**. Upgrade your life with the rest."*
