## Shot-by-Shot Art Direction & Choreography

### Scene 1: The Illusion (Frames 0 – 255 / 0.0s – 8.5s)
- **Visual World:** The Warm Ivory Gallery (`#FAF8F2`)
- **Lighting:** Soft directional key light from upper-left with warm ambient fill and realistic contact drop shadows.
- **Focal Visual:** Macro push-in on a 560px precision-rendered \$100 banknote centered on an architectural plinth.
  - Guilloché lathe curves, fine crosshatch micro-engraving lines, crisp "100" numerical denomination, and an iridescent holographic vertical security ribbon.
  - At t = 1.8s – 2.4s, a golden specular light sweep rolls diagonally across the note, catching a diamond caustic starburst glint on the rim at t = 2.0s.
- **Typography:**
  - Hero headline: `IS YOUR MONEY LOSING ITS POWER?` (88px, `#141416`, font-weight 800, animated clip-path wipe from left to right).
  - No decorative corner tags, no "VOL 01", no card borders. Pure cinematic focus.
- **Transition:** Seamless spatial scale-up through the note into Scene 2.

### Scene 2: Same Money, Different World (Frames 255 – 555 / 8.5s – 18.5s)
- **Visual World:** Temporal Vitrine Evolution (Daylight Sand `#EAE6DD` → High-Tech Obsidian Studio `#161920`)
- **Lighting:** Smooth 1.5s lighting transition from warm daylight into cool, moody cinematic rim lighting.
- **Focal Visual:** A physical glass vitrine display on a floating stone base.
  - **Phase 1 (Today, t = 8.5s – 12.0s):** The \$100 banknote rests in the center, perfectly matched to a modular 3D collection of tangible goods (stylized geometric canisters: daily groceries, utility units, transportation badge). A clear boundary holds them in 1:1 balance.
  - **Phase 2 (Future Shift, t = 12.0s – 18.5s):** The lighting cools into deep slate. The physical \$100 banknote remains completely identical in size, sharpness, and appearance. But the price boundary of that full basket expands upward to \$180.61. The portion purchasable by the single \$100 note contracts to a compact inner boundary (55%), while the unattainable 45% dims into a translucent ghosted wireframe.
- **Typography:**
  - Hero motion header: `THE SAME BILL. A DIFFERENT REALITY.` (92px, dynamic contrast).
  - Supporting line: `PRICES MOVE. PAPER DOES NOT.` (56px, tracked uppercase).
- **Transition:** Cut to the macroeconomic mechanics of Scene 3.

### Scene 3: Understanding Inflation (Frames 555 – 930 / 18.5s – 31.0s)
- **Visual World:** The Kinetic Bronze Workshop (`#110E0C` with golden rim accents)
- **Lighting:** High-contrast overhead spotlight illuminating a precision mechanical balancing scale.
- **Focal Visual:** An isometric mechanical balance beam with brass fulcrum and dual suspended plates.
  - **Left Plate:** A fixed, engraved solid gold bullion weight: `NOMINAL VALUE: $100` (flat, constant mass).
  - **Right Plate:** The `GENERAL PRICE LEVEL` weight. As the scene progresses, annual inflation pulses swell the right weight: 1.00× → 1.34× → 1.80×.
  - The scale physically tilts under the expanding price mass. The nominal \$100 arm is forced downward relative to purchasing equilibrium.
  - Animated callout rings emphasize the core rule: Inflation is the increase in the general price level across the whole economy, even while individual bank balances remain static.
- **Typography:**
  - Hero headline: `INFLATION IS A RISING TIDE.` (84px, `#F5F2EB`).
  - Kinetic sub-phrase: `NOMINAL VALUE VS REAL PURCHASING POWER` (46px, `#E5A93C`).
- **Transition:** Rapid camera whip into the digital observatory coordinate grid of Scene 4.
