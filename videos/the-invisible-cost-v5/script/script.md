## Core Creative Principle
> **ONE SHOT. ONE IDEA. ONE CLEAR VISUAL FOCUS.**  
> *A single continuous light studio environment with cardless physical object analogies that make inflation instantly intuitive to a curious 10-year-old.*

---

## Shot-by-Shot Script & Action Ledger

### Shot 1 — The Money (0.0s – 8.0s / Frames 0 – 240)
- **Spoken Narration (starts at t = 0.4s):**  
  > *"Imagine you have one hundred dollars."*
- **Visual Focus:**  
  - 3D $100 Federal Reserve Note resting on an illuminated warm ivory floor with authentic contact shadow.
  - Guilloché engravings, blue micro-printed security ribbon, rolling specular light sweep, and caustic starburst glint.
  - Monumental statement: `THIS IS $100.` (92px serif).
  - Unbroken perspective floor grid compressing toward the horizon. Zero subtitles, zero dashboard cards.

---

### Shot 2 — What It Buys (8.0s – 18.0s / Frames 240 – 540)
- **Spoken Narration (starts at t = 8.5s):**  
  > *"Today, it can buy you a basket of things you need."*
- **Visual Focus:**  
  - Artisanal wicker grocery basket filled to 100% capacity with bread, milk, oats, apples, and eggs.
  - Subtly hovering beside the $100 note to establish direct 1-to-1 purchasing equivalence.
  - Monumental statement: `TODAY, IT BUYS THIS.` (86px serif).
  - Single architectural plinth: `$100 ➔ 100% OF THIS BASKET`.

---

### Shot 3 — Time Passes (18.0s – 35.0s / Frames 540 – 1050)
- **Spoken Narration (starts at t = 18.4s):**  
  > *"Now imagine coming back twenty years later. The same basket costs much more. But your hundred-dollar note still says one hundred dollars. Why? Because prices have been rising little by little, year after year. That's inflation."*
- **Visual Focus:**  
  - Clean polished brass balance scale on the continuous ivory studio floor.
  - Left pan holds the unchanged $100 note. Right pan holds expanding terracotta price weight (+80.6%).
  - Beam tilts realistically up to 12° under the weight of rising prices.
  - Studio pill ticker smoothly rolls: `YEAR 0 OF 20` ➔ `YEAR 20 OF 20`.
  - Monumental statement: `PRICES RISE. THE BILL DOESN'T.` (78px serif).

---

### Shot 4 — The Same Basket (35.0s – 47.0s / Frames 1050 – 1410)
- **Spoken Narration (starts at t = 35.4s):**  
  > *"Let's say prices rise by 3% every year. After twenty years, that same basket would cost about 181 dollars."*
- **Visual Focus:**  
  - The exact same wicker grocery basket returns to center stage at 100% fill.
  - A swinging physical parchment price tag attached to the basket handle reveals the price compounding up from `$100.00` to `$180.61`.
  - Amber border with crisp bronze typography on warm parchment, maintaining 100% light theme compliance.
  - Monumental statement: `THE SAME BASKET NOW COSTS MORE.` (78px serif).

---

### Shot 5 — The Realization (47.0s – 59.0s / Frames 1410 – 1770)
- **Spoken Narration (starts at t = 47.4s):**  
  > *"So your 100 dollars would now buy only about 55% of that basket. Your money didn't disappear. It just can't buy as much as before."*
- **Visual Focus:**  
  - The exact same wicker grocery basket visibly ghosts out 44.6% of its items, physically demonstrating that only 55.4% fits within the original $100 budget.
  - Single integrated plinth readout: `$100 NOTE ➔ 55.4% OF THE BASKET ($55.37 PURCHASING VALUE)`.
  - Supporting clarification: *"Your money didn't disappear. It just buys less than before."*
  - Monumental statement: `YOUR $100 NOW BUYS ABOUT 55%.` (78px serif with emerald accent).

---

### Shot 6 — The Conclusion (59.0s – 69.0s / Frames 1770 – 2070)
- **Spoken Narration (starts at t = 59.4s):**  
  > *"That's why the number in your wallet isn't the whole story. What that money can buy matters too."*
- **Visual Focus:**  
  - Sculptural monetary seal medallion centered in the warm ivory architectural gallery.
  - Progressive disclosure of the core thesis:
    - Phase 1 (59.0s – 63.5s): `THE NUMBER STAYED THE SAME.` (78px primary text).
    - Phase 2 (63.5s – 69.0s): `WHAT IT CAN BUY CHANGED.` (78px warm amber text) + `NEVER MISTAKE THE NUMBER FOR THE PURCHASING POWER.` (34px uppercase subphrase).
  - Mobile-readable regulatory disclaimer anchored at bottom (24px, -apple-system, 0.85 opacity):
    *"Hypothetical illustration: $100 with 3% annual compound inflation over 20 years. Individual prices and actual inflation rates vary. Educational analysis only, not financial advice."*
